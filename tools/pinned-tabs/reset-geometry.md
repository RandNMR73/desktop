# Changed pinned-tab geometry

Navigating within a pinned tab used to move its title from x=44 to x=49⅓ points. The title now stays at x=44 and the favicon at x=18. The reset control occupies a 36 × 36-point rectangle beginning at x=8, with 10-point horizontal padding. The first geometry correction used a 28-point control plus an 8-point margin; the current geometry preserves that alignment while correcting the hover and click footprint.

Arc 1.171.0 (89666), binary SHA-256 `e82d2a4245b6239860e41a149cb31c0c030e35b383456544c81f21d45ee03376`, provides the read-only reference. `TabCellView`’s slash factory at `0x100369ba4` sets a 4 × 15-point view. `SlashView` drawing at `0x100a445d8` uses a 2-point round stroke from (1,1) to (width−1,height−1) in AppKit coordinates. The CSS mask maps that path into top-down coordinates. The `TabCellSlash` asset resolves to black at 0.3 opacity in Aqua and white at 0.3 in Dark Aqua. The regional corner-radius getter at `0x102a68d0c` returns 12.

Native Arc inspection used an owned localhost pin before and after navigation and in the favicon region. The title remains aligned; the separator lies between favicon and title. The fixture was unpinned and closed afterward, and Home / X plus the original Little Arc Settings window were restored.

Read-only tracing of `TabCellView.layout` at `0x10035d49c` gives the reset width as twice the favicon's inset within the selection background plus the favicon width: 2 × 10 + 16 = 36 points. The title region begins at the separator's x=36, overlapping the reset frame by eight points. Arc's ordered rectangular hit test gives the overlap to the reset region. Transparent CSS hit surfaces preserve the rectangular corners and exclude the two-point outer gutters without covering the title or unload control.

`TabCellRegionalBackgroundButton.updateLayer` at `0x1000addf4` uses SidebarItemBackground while hovered and TabCellBackgroundPressed while pressed. The hover asset is Display P3 (0.055, 0.059, 0.063) at 5% in Aqua and (0.98, 0.984, 1) at 10% in Dark Aqua. The pressed asset uses those same components at 6.5% in both appearances. Neutral reset and title regions have all four 12-point corners. Selected, multiselected, and dragged pins retain the base selection background, expose only the reset region, and round its left corners. Forced colors retain the existing system hover paint; Favorites, split views, and Glance tabs are excluded.

Validation covers 160 color/layout checks, 60 hit-target checks, and 80 hover-animation regression checks across all five Spaces in a disposable headed browser. Full pixel equivalence, split/group combinations, simultaneous provider/developer subtitles, and a speed advantage remain unverified. Historical separator evidence remains in Astralforge's `tools/zen-bench/results/pinned-reset-geometry-2026-10-30`; current regional evidence is in `tools/zen-bench/results/pinned-region-colors-2026-10-30`.
